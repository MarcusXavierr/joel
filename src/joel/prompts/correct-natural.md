natural: start from grammatical and change only a word or fixed phrase that a native speaker would never use there, such as "made a party" to "threw a party" or "said a lie" to "told a lie".
Replace those words with what a native speaker would say, and touch nothing else.
Never add or delete words to shorten, polish, or change the register.
If nothing sounds wrong that way, return grammatical unchanged.
In the example, natural for 3 is "I threw a party last week."; natural for 1 and 2 equals grammatical.
